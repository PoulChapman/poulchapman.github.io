How to play WordMaster

  1. Guess the hidden five-letter word before you run out of attempts.
  2. Type five letters, then press Enter or select Submit Guess.
  3. The green circle counts letters that are correct and in the correct place.
  4. The yellow circle counts letters that are in the word but in the wrong place.
  5. Guess boxes are never colored for you. Click each box to add an independent note: light yellow, light green, light red, then clear.
  6. Used Letters shows which A–Z letters have appeared in submitted guesses.
  7. Helper Mode blocks letters you marked red on the board.
  8. Attempts range from 5 to 15 and lock once you submit your first guess of a round.
  9. Run out of attempts and you can choose to keep trying with endless rounds.
